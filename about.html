Our Story

A neighbourhood kitchen, built on fresh ingredients

The Bake House Pune began in 2018 as a small home kitchen with one oven and a simple promise: nothing leaves our counter unless it's baked that same day. Eight years on, that promise hasn't changed.

Every cake is layered by hand, every loaf is shaped and proved the traditional way, and every batch of pastry dough is laminated fresh each morning. We source our butter, cocoa and fruit carefully, and we never keep anything on the shelf longer than it should be.

Our kitchen follows strict hygiene protocols at every stage — from ingredient storage to packaging — because the people of Pune who trust us with their celebrations deserve nothing less. It's this attention to quality and cleanliness that keeps our customers, and their families, coming back.
